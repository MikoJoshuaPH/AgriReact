Help Center

How do I sell a product?

Click the "What's on your mind?" box, choose "Sell Product", fill in the details and post. An admin reviews it before it appears in the Marketplace.

Where can I see my pending posts?

Open "Pending Posts" in the sidebar. Posts stay there until an administrator approves or deletes them.

How do I check out?

Add products to your cart, tick the items you want, and press Checkout. Your order appears under My Orders.

Need more help?

Send a complaint or report using the form below. Your account name and email are attached automatically.
