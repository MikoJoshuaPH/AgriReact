Help Center

How do I sell a product?

Farmers can list products. Listings and posts appear immediately; users can report a post for administrator review.

What can buyers post?

Buyers can share text and photo/video posts, but cannot create product listings.

When are my posts visible?

Your posts are published immediately. Administrators may review reports from users and remove content that violates community guidelines.

How do I check out?

Add products to your cart, tick the items you want, and press Checkout. Your order appears under My Orders.

Need more help?

Send a complaint or report using the form below. Your account name and email are attached automatically.
